How links are built

Playlist, stream, and EPG links reuse the token/API key and region filters shown at the top.

  • Streams play via /stream/<id> and optional ?variant=<name>.
  • HLS manifests live at /hls/<id>/index.m3u8 and DASH at /dash/<id>/manifest.mpd.
  • Status is polled from /api/status; recordings use /api/streams/<id>/recordings/*.
